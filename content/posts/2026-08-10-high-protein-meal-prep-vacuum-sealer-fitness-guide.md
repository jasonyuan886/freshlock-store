Whether you are building muscle, cutting fat, or maintaining a performance diet, consistent protein intake is non-negotiable. ## Why Vacuum Sealing Is Perfect for High-Protein Meal Prep

Protein-rich foods like chicken breast, lean beef, fish, and eggs are perishable and expensive. Vacuum sealing gives you three key advantages:

1. **Extended shelf life:** Vacuum-sealed chicken lasts 2–3 weeks in the fridge vs. 2–3 days in regular packaging. See our [complete food shelf life guide](/blog/how-long-does-vacuum-sealed-food-last) for full details.
2. **Portion precision:** Pre-portion protein into exact macro counts, seal individually, and grab what you need.
3. **Freezer burn prevention:** Frozen chicken and fish are especially vulnerable to freezer burn. Vacuum sealing eliminates this. Learn more in our [freezer burn prevention guide](/blog/how-to-prevent-freezer-burn-vacuum-sealing).

## Protein-Specific Vacuum Sealing Tips

### Chicken Breast
- **Raw:** Seal individually or in pairs. Freeze for up to 9 months.
- **Cooked:** Cool completely, seal in portions. Freeze for up to 3 months.
- **Marinated:** Vacuum sealing actually accelerates marination — check our [vacuum sealing marinades guide](/blog/vacuum-sealing-marinades-guide).

### Fish and Seafood
- Pre-freeze fillets for 30 minutes before sealing to prevent crushing.
- Vacuum-sealed salmon stays fresh 1–2 weeks in fridge vs. 1–2 days normally.
- See our dedicated [fish and seafood storage guide](/blog/vacuum-sealer-for-fish-and-seafood-storage).

### Eggs
- Hard-boiled eggs (peeled) vacuum seal well and last 5–7 days in the fridge.
- Do NOT vacuum seal raw eggs in shells — the pressure will crack them.
- Liquid egg whites can be frozen in vacuum bags (lay flat for stacking).

### Lean Beef
- Portion raw ground beef before freezing for easy defrosting.
- Cooked beef portions last 2 weeks vacuum-sealed in fridge.
- For marinating, vacuum seal steak with marinade for 30 minutes = 4 hours of regular marinating.
