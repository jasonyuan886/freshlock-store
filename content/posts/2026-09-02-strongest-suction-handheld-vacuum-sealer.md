## What Does "Strongest Suction" Actually Mean?

Vacuum sealer suction is measured in **kilopascals (kPa)**. The more negative the number, the stronger the vacuum:

- **-30 kPa** — Basic handheld sealers, removes ~70% of air
- **-45 kPa** — Mid-range models, removes ~80% of air
- **-60 kPa** — Strongest in class, removes ~95% of air

**The difference is dramatic.** At -60 kPa, you remove 35% more air than a -30 kPa sealer. That translates directly to:

- **2-3x longer freshness** for meat, fish, and produce
- **Near-zero freezer burn** on long-term frozen storage
- **Better marinades** — stronger vacuum pulls seasoning deeper into meat in minutes
