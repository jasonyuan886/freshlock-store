# FreshLock Pro vs Starter Kit: Which Should You Buy?

If you've landed on the FreshLock product page, you've probably noticed two options: the standalone **FreshLock Pro ($74.99)** and the **Starter Kit ($94.99)**. Here's exactly what the $20 difference gets you, and which one makes sense for your situation.

## What's Actually Different

| | FreshLock Pro | Starter Kit |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $74.99 | $94.99 |
| FreshLock Pro sealer | ✅ | ✅ |
| Vacuum bags included | 0 | 30 bags |
| USB-C charging cable | ✅ | ✅ |
| -60 kPa suction | ✅ | ✅ |
| Warranty | 1 year | 1 year |

The sealer itself is identical in both — same -60 kPa suction, same USB-C charging, same 210g compact build. The only difference is whether 30 vacuum bags are bundled in.

## The Math on Bags

A 30-pack of vacuum seal bags sells separately for **$29.99**. Buying the Starter Kit bundle at $94.99 (sealer + 30 bags) instead of buying the Pro ($74.99) and a 30-pack separately ($29.99, totaling $104.98) saves **roughly $10** — plus you're not left with an unused sealer waiting on a bag delivery.

## Which One Should You Buy

**Get the Starter Kit if:**
- This is your first vacuum sealer and you don't already own compatible embossed valve bags
- You want to start using it same-day without a separate bag order
- You'd rather save the ~$10 bundle discount

**Get the FreshLock Pro alone if:**
- You already have embossed valve bags from a previous sealer (the Pro works with any standard embossed valve bag, not just FreshLock-branded ones)
- You plan to buy bags in bulk anyway (the 50-pack at $39.99 works out to a lower per-bag cost than the 30-pack)
- You're buying as a gift and the recipient likely already has bags, or you'd rather they choose their own bag size

## A Note on Bag Compatibility

Neither option locks you into FreshLock bags specifically — the Pro works with **any standard embossed valve bag** from any brand. That means the 30 bags in the Starter Kit are a convenience, not a requirement; you can always restock from any source once they run out.

## Bottom Line

For most first-time buyers, the **Starter Kit is the better value** — it's cheaper than buying both pieces separately and gets you sealing on day one. If you're upgrading from another sealer and already have a bag supply, the standalone Pro saves you from paying for bags you don't need yet.
