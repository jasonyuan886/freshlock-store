# Best Vacuum Sealer for Restaurants and Small Food Businesses

Commercial chamber vacuum sealers start around $500 and can run into the thousands. Before you spend that, it's worth being honest about whether your volume actually requires one — a lot of small food operations are better served by a handheld sealer, at least at first.

## When a Handheld Sealer Is the Right Tool

- **Small-batch meal prep businesses** sealing 10-40 portions a day — a handheld sealer at seconds-per-bag keeps pace without a five-figure equipment investment
- **Home-based catering or bakeries** portioning ingredients or finished items ahead of events
- **Food trucks and pop-ups** with limited counter space and no dedicated prep kitchen for a countertop chamber unit
- **Sous vide-forward restaurants** sealing individual protein portions for cook-to-order service, where speed per bag matters more than sealing bulk liquid marinades

## When You Actually Need a Commercial Chamber Sealer

- You're sealing **liquids and marinades in bulk** — chamber sealers vacuum the entire chamber (including the open bag), so liquids don't get pulled into the pump the way they can with an external, clip-on sealer
- You're processing **50+ bags per shift** — the per-bag speed of a chamber sealer's assembly-line workflow pulls ahead of a handheld at high volume
- You need **health-department-rated commercial equipment** for a licensed commercial kitchen (check your local regulations — some jurisdictions require NSF-certified equipment for commercial food service)
