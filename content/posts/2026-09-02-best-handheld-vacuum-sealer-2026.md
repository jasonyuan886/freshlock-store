# Best Handheld Vacuum Sealer 2026: 5 Things That Actually Matter

Every "best vacuum sealer" list online is the same: they list 10 products, most of them countertop models, half are out of stock, and none explain *why* one is better than another.

This isn't that list. Here are the **5 specs that actually determine** whether a handheld vacuum sealer will work for you — and which model nails all five.

## 1. Suction Power (The One Spec That Matters Most)

Not all vacuum sealers pull the same amount of air out of the bag. The measurement is **kilopascals (kPa)** — and it is the difference between food lasting 2 days vs. 2 weeks in the fridge.

- **Below -30 kPa:** Barely better than manually squeezing air out. Food lasts maybe 20% longer.
- **-30 to -45 kPa:** Decent for short-term storage. Fine for leftovers you will eat this week.
- **-60 kPa (strongest suction available):** Removes ~95% of air. Food lasts 3-5x longer. Real freezer burn protection.

The **FreshLock Pro at -60 kPa** has the strongest suction of any handheld vacuum sealer on the market.

## 2. Bag Compatibility (Do Not Get Locked In)

Some brands sell you a cheap sealer and then charge $25 for 100 proprietary bags. The FreshLock Pro works with **any embossed valve bag** from any brand.

What to look for:
- **Standard one-way air valve** (not a proprietary shape)
- **90um or thicker** PA+PE film (thinner bags leak)
- **Embossed channel** for air extraction

When sealing juicy foods, liquid gets pulled toward the nozzle. Without protection, it enters the motor and kills the pump.

The FreshLock Pro has a **detachable transparent drip tray** that catches liquid before it reaches the motor.
