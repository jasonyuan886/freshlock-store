## The five rules of kitchen gifting

- Buy **tools, not gadgets** — if it plugs in and only does one thing, be suspicious.
- **Consumables are safe** — good olive oil, real vanilla, proper salt. Always welcome.
- **Upgrades beat novelties** — a better version of something they use daily beats a new thing entirely.
- **Pairs beat singles** — sealer + jar kit, circulator + bags. Gifts that complete a system get remembered.
- **Receipts, always.** Even winners sometimes duplicate.
